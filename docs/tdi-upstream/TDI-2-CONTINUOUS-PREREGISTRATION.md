# TDI-2 — Preregistration of the continuous evaluation

Freeze date: 12 July 2026<br>
Branch: `tdi-2-branching`

## 1. Motivation

The initial binary objective defined recovery as an exact distributional
equality at horizon 6.

After excluding systems already recovered at observation horizon 2,
this objective becomes degenerate:

- training: 1 positive case out of 12,000;
- holdout: 0 positive cases out of 4,000;
- AUPRC not interpretable;
- TDI-2 binary criterion failed.

The exhaustive width-2 distribution nevertheless shows 44,300 systems with
partial final recovery out of 50,625. The continuous recovery variable
therefore contains much more information than the rare event
"recovery exactly equal to 1".

## 2. Main question

Do the prospective features conditioned on the intervention, observed up to
horizon 2, predict distributional recovery at horizon 6 beyond a non-TDI
baseline using the same temporal budget?

## 3. Population

### Training

- width: 3;
- 12,000 accepted systems;
- seeds starting from 0;
- transitions generated by `SplitMix64`;
- each state has a non-empty set of successors;
- uniform local choice among the distinct successors.

### Main holdout

- width: 3;
- 4,000 accepted systems;
- seeds starting from 1,000,000;
- no use during design or tuning.

### Out-of-distribution holdout

- width: 4;
- 4,000 accepted systems;
- seeds starting from 2,000,000;
- same protocol, larger state space.

## 4. Intervention and horizons

- reference state: zero state;
- perturbation: flip of bit `width - 1`;
- dynamical action: `Noop`;
- observation horizon: 2;
- outcome horizon: 6.

Systems whose recovery is already exactly 1 at horizon 2 are excluded from
the main analysis, because their future recovery is then determined exactly
by the Markov property.

No other system is excluded.

## 5. Target variable

The continuous target is the exact recovery at horizon 6:

\[
O_6 = \sum_x \min(P_6(x), Q_6(x)).
\]

It belongs to the interval `[0, 1]` and equals `1 - TV(P_6,Q_6)`.

Exact rationals are preserved during propagation. Conversion to `f64`
occurs only for training and metrics.

## 6. Paired baseline

The baseline may only use information available up to horizon 2:

- entropy of reference paths at depths 1 and 2;
- entropy of perturbed paths at depths 1 and 2;
- number of reachable states at depths 1 and 2;
- number of paths at depths 1 and 2;
- same measures for the perturbed trajectories.

It may not use:

- any overlap between distributions;
- any data from a horizon greater than 2;
- any attractor or descriptor computed on the full future;
- any information derived from the target.

## 7. TDI-2 features

The challenger model adds to the baseline:

- exact recovery at depth 1;
- exact recovery at depth 2;
- variation `O₂ - O₁`.

These features represent the prospective geometry of the response to the
intervention, not a complete orbital property.

## 8. Predictive model

Both models use exactly the same algorithm:

- deterministic ridge regression;
- standardization computed only on the training set;
- unpenalized constant term;
- regularization coefficient fixed at `λ = 1`;
- deterministic solution of the normal equations;
- predictions bounded in `[0,1]`.

No hyperparameter will be tuned on the holdouts.

## 9. Metrics

### Primary

- mean squared error, MSE.

### Secondary

- mean absolute error, MAE;
- coefficient of determination `R²`;
- Spearman rank correlation.

## 10. Uncertainty

A deterministic paired bootstrap of 2,000 replications will measure:

- MSE improvement: `MSE_baseline - MSE_TDI`;
- MAE improvement: `MAE_baseline - MAE_TDI`.

Bootstrap seed:

`0x5444_4932_434F_4E54`

## 11. Primary success criterion

TDI-2 is declared successful on the main holdout only if:

1. the observed relative reduction of MSE is greater than or equal to 5 %;
2. the lower bound of the 95 % bootstrap interval of the MSE improvement
   is strictly positive;
3. the lower bound of the 95 % bootstrap interval of the MAE improvement
   is strictly positive.

The width-4 holdout is confirmatory. It must show a positive MSE
improvement, but its interval is not included in the primary criterion.

## 12. Negative interpretation

The result will be declared negative if:

- the primary criterion fails;
- the gain disappears against the paired baseline;
- the performance comes only from systems almost or already recovered;
- the gain does not reproduce on the out-of-distribution holdout.

No threshold, horizon, sample or criterion will be modified after the first
computation of the main holdout.
